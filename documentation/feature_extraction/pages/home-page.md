## 6. States aur Variations
- **Header Cart Badge:** Active state shows count `2` (`bg-[#ff3f6c]`). Design me empty 0-count state specified nahi.
- **Top Ranking Tabs:** Active state has dark background/pill styling (`All`), inactive has neutral background.
- **Stock Bar:** Color coded progress bar showing % claimed.
- **VIP Vault Card:** Locked state badge `VIP ACCESS ONLY`, `Unlocks at Tier 3 Loyalty`.
- **Carousel Arrows:** Left arrow disabled/hidden on first slide: Design mein specified nahi.
- **Logged-in vs Guest:** Design shows general authenticated utility icons with active cart count `2`.

## 7. Visual & Styling Notes
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Pink/Coral Accent: `#ff3f6c` (Logo, cart counter badge)
  - Crimson Urgent Red: `#ba1a1a` (`NEW` badge, urgent countdown highlights)
  - Telegram Blue: `#229ED9`
  - Background Neutral: Light gray `#f9fafb` to pure white `#ffffff`
  - Borders: `#e5e7eb` and `#f2eded`
- **Typography:** Modern clean sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), high contrast bold display headings (`Playfair Display` for editorial headers).
- **Border Radius:**
  - Full capsules (`rounded-full`) for search bar, store tags, and category pills.
  - Medium cards (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`) for product cards and banners.
- **Button Variants:**
  - Solid Black Pill (`bg-black text-white hover:bg-neutral-800`)
  - Outline Border Pill (`border border-black text-black`)
  - Coral Solid (`bg-[#ff3f6c] text-white`)
