### 2.6. Left Main Column: Policy Articles Breakdown

#### Part A: Privacy Policy Articles (Articles 1 to 6)
1. **Article 1: Data Collection & Minimalist Gathering (`01`):**
   - Tag: `Mandatory Disclosure`
   - Data Minimization Rule: Sirf wahi telemetry collect ki jati hai jo luxury tailoring, private delivery, aur tax compliance ke liye anivarya hai.
   - Categories:
     - `Identity Telemetry`: Legal name, contact address, email, telephone number.
     - `Bespoke Sizing Telemetry`: Optional measurements, fit notes, preserved securely in vault profile.
     - `Encrypted Payment Tokens`: Payment card details kabhi store nahi hote; tokenized strings PCI-DSS Level 1 vaults me process hote hain.
     - `System Logs`: Anonymized IP geography, device browser agent for fraud prevention.
2. **Article 2: Use of Personal Data & Fulfillment (`02`):**
   - Tag: `Legitimate Interest`
   - Data sirf order updates, bespoke tailoring verification, tracking alerts, aur discreet unboxing advisory ke liye use hota hai.
   - **Zero Data Broker Policy:** Customer data kabhi third-party advertisers ko sell ya lease nahi kiya jata.
3. **Article 3: Cookies, Tracking & Preferences (`03`):**
   - Tag: `User Controlled`
   - Essential session cookies mandatory hain; analytics cookies optional hain aur user instant preference toggle se disable kar sakta hai.
4. **Article 4: Third-Party Integrations & Vault Encryption (`04`):**
   - Tag: `256-bit SSL`
   - End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, 256-bit AES safeguards, SOC 2 Type II certified cloud database environments.
5. **Article 5: Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Erasure) (`05`):**
   - Global consumer rights: Right to Access, Right to Portability, Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), Right to Opt-Out.
6. **Article 6: Data Retention & Security Safeguards (`06`):**
   - Order audit logs legal statutory requirement tak retain hote hain; idle profiles 24 months ke baad anonymize ho jati hain.

#### Part B: White-Glove Shipping & Logistics Standards
1. **SD-1: Delivery Speed Tiers & Global Despatch:**
   - `Express Dispatch`: 1–2 Business Days (Priority air delivery with dedicated courier handoff).
   - `Standard Atelier Ground`: 3–5 Business Days (Zero-emission localized freight transport with carbon offset).
   - `International Courier`: 4–7 Business Days (Pre-cleared DDP customs clearance across 40+ global territories).
   - **Discreet Packaging Rule:** Tamper-evident, unbranded outer cartons with zero external item descriptions or brand tags.
2. **SD-2: Signature Verification & GPS Telemetry:**
   - High-value collections kabhi porches ya unattended mailrooms me drop nahi kiye jate; direct physical sign-off ya SMS OTP mandatory hai.
   - Real-time encrypted GPS waypoint tracking available from Paris atelier to destination.
3. **Lost Parcel & Address Amendments Protocol:**
   - Dedicated investigation team with 48-hour resolution SLA and instant complimentary dispatch if lost in transit.

#### Part C: Reimbursement & Refund Mechanics
1. **RF-1: Fiat vs Crypto Reimbursement Mechanics:**
   - **Fiat Reimbursements (USD, EUR, GBP, INR):** Card/Bank/UPI handle me 3–5 business days me reverse credit.
   - **Cryptocurrency Settlements (USDT, USDC, BTC):** Original transaction USD value ke equivalent stablecoin transfer ya Store Credit (+5% bonus reward voucher).
   - Gas fees aur card processing fees complimentary domestic returns par store bear karta hai.
2. **RF-2: Settlement Timelines & Non-Refundable Items:**
   - **Intimate Wellness Hygiene Rule:** Items marked as custom bespoke monograms or **unsealed intimate apparel / wellness devices** are strictly exempt from return refunds due to international health, sanitary, and hygiene irreversibility once the holographic factory seal is breached.

#### Part D: Doorstep Return & Exchange Execution
1. **30-Day Complimentary Doorstep Pickup:**
   - Courier brings pre-printed label and tamper-proof courier satchel directly to client residence.
2. **Garment Condition Criteria:**
   - Unworn, unwashed, unaltered with original designer provenance security tags attached.
