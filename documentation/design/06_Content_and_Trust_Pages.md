## 📜 4. Comprehensive Policy Pages

### A. Discreet Packaging & Shipping Policy (`/policies/shipping`)
* **Exterior Box:** Plain recycled corrugated brown cardboard box with standard tamper-proof brown tape.
* **Waybill & Consignor:** Shipper name is legally declared as *"B-Dot Logistics / B-Dot Technologies"*. No product pictures, logos, or intimate descriptions are ever printed.
* **Zero Voice Call Embarrassment:** Delivery partners are instructed to follow standard parcel delivery without questioning or revealing box contents.

### B. Hygiene & 48-Hour Video DOA Policy (`/policies/returns-warranty`)
* **Hygiene Non-Returnable Clause:** In compliance with international sanitary health standards, any intimate device that has had its sterile hygiene seal opened cannot be returned for a change of mind.
* **48-Hour Unconditional DOA (Dead-on-Arrival) Replacement:**
  * Customers are advised to record a quick unboxing video showing the packaging and motor test.
  * If the device is non-functional or damaged in transit, uploading the video clip guarantees an instant free replacement dispatched within 24 hours, without requiring the customer to return the intimate device.
