# 👥 Project Users & Roles: Discreet Adult Wellness & Intimate Care Marketplace

> **Purpose:** Identify all user categories, personas, operational personnel, and automated system entities that interact with or operate this platform.
> 
> *Note on Scope:* Detailed screen-by-screen access permission matrices and database schema rules are mapped in later technical phases. Here, the focus is deeply defining **who** touches the platform, their behavioral psychology, their **core operational purpose**, and their **end-to-end relationship** with the system.

---

## 📋 Comprehensive Roles Catalog

---

### Role 1: Primary Consumer & Discreet Buyer (Solo Explorers & Couples)
- **Role Name:** Consumer / Discreet Buyer
- **Who They Are:** 
  Urban and Tier-1/Tier-2 Indian adults (aged 21–40), primarily including modern women, self-care seekers, progressive individuals, and couples looking to enhance relationship intimacy. They browse predominantly on private mobile devices during late evening personal downtime. They carry high privacy anxiety regarding societal taboo, family or roommate discovery, and courier judgment.
- **Core Personas within this Role:**
  1. *The Anxious First-Timer (Beginner):* Hesitant, curious, and fearful of bodily harm or low-quality materials. Needs gentle educational guidance, medical certification assurances, and foolproof discretion.
  2. *The Conscious Wellness Explorer:* Experienced shopper prioritizing premium, body-safe materials (medical-grade platinum silicone, phthalate-free certifications, whisper-quiet motors) over cheap novelties.
  3. *The Couple / Romantic Partner:* Searching for shared intimacy toys or purchasing curated anniversary/romantic gifts with complete assurance of blind packaging.
- **Authentication & Access Modes:**
  - *Guest Checkout Mode:* High-speed checkout using mobile/email OTP verification without mandatory password creation or permanent public footprint.
  - *Discreet Vault Account Mode:* Passwordless OTP or optional Google/Email OAuth login for repeat buyers desiring an encrypted, discreet order history, address storage, and seamless warranty claims.
- **Core Purpose (Why they use the platform):**
  - Safely discover and purchase certified body-safe adult toys, wellness devices, and companion care consumables (organic water-based lubes, antibacterial foaming cleaners) without shame, fear of toxic chemicals, or risk of exposure.
  - Utilize interactive discovery tools (such as the 3-question Toy Finder Quiz and 24/7 AI Wellness Assistant) to find products matching their anatomy, experience level, and comfort boundaries.
  - Enjoy peace of mind via the 1-click Quick Exit / Panic Button, neutral bank statement billing descriptors, plain tamper-evident packaging, and silent post-purchase support.
- **General Relationship to the Project:**
  - Passes discreet 1-click 18+ age verification upon arrival.
  - Interacts with dynamic product catalog, filtering by noise levels (<50dB), waterproof ratings (IPX7), and material certifications.
  - Engages with the anonymous Toy Finder Quiz and confidential AI Wellness Chatbot.
  - Executes transactions via frictionless prepaid payment (UPI/Cards) or COD with automated OTP verification.
  - Tracks shipment progress via generic, non-explicit SMS/Email notifications.
  - Accesses confidential self-service warranty claim portal to submit unboxing videos in case of dead-on-arrival (DOA) defects.

### Role 2: Customer Support & Intimate Care Specialist
- **Role Name:** Support & Intimate Care Specialist
- **Who They Are:** 
  Trained internal support representatives and intimate wellness advisors equipped with product material knowledge and empathetic, sex-positive, non-judgmental customer service protocols.
- **Core Purpose (Why they use the platform):**
  - Provide confidential, text-based pre-sales and post-sales support without ever making intrusive or unsolicited telephone calls to customers.
  - Answer sensitive questions regarding product usage, silicone maintenance, charging protocols, and compatibility with lubricants.
  - Review and adjudicate customer warranty claims under the strict hygiene policy (evaluating unboxing videos for motor defects, missing components, or transit damage while upholding the non-returnable policy for opened personal goods).
- **General Relationship to the Project:**
  - Operates a secure, role-restricted customer support desk.
  - Manages ticket escalations routed from the automated AI Wellness Chatbot.
  - Evaluates uploaded unboxing media within an encrypted viewer to approve or reject replacement dispatches.
  - Initiates replacement orders through the backend without exposing customer PII or requiring telephone interaction.

### Role 3: Warehouse & Logistics Fulfillment Coordinator
- **Role Name:** Fulfillment & Packaging Operations Associate
- **Who They Are:** 
  Warehouse associates and dispatch personnel responsible for physical inventory handling, order packaging, quality inspection, and courier handover.
- **Core Purpose (Why they use the platform):**
  - Execute 100% blind packaging standards to eliminate delivery shame and leakage.
  - Verify that every dispatched parcel utilizes unmarked, heavy-duty brown cardboard boxes, tamper-evident seals, and completely generic shipping labels displaying only the neutral sender entity name (*"Aura Logistics"*).
  - Ensure zero product names, SKU descriptions, or adult wellness terminology appear on outward-facing shipping manifests, invoices, or courier waybills.
- **General Relationship to the Project:**
  - Accesses the dedicated fulfillment queue on the warehouse dashboard.
  - Scans product barcodes during packing to verify correct item and accessory inclusion (e.g., magnetic USB charger, storage pouch).
  - Generates and prints neutral shipping labels integrated directly with 3PL logistics carriers (Shiprocket/Delhivery).
  - Flags suspicious, unverified, or incomplete COD orders for automated pre-dispatch confirmation before physical boxing.

### Role 4: Catalog Merchandiser & Brand Partner Manager
- **Role Name:** Catalog & Vendor Operations Manager
- **Who They Are:** 
  Internal commercial category managers who manage relationships with authorized domestic and international wellness manufacturers (e.g., Satisfyer, Lelo, Womanizer, MyMuse) and companion consumable brands.
- **Core Purpose (Why they use the platform):**
  - Maintain a verified, multi-brand catalog adhering to the highest global medical safety standards.
  - Ensure all product listings feature rigorous material disclosures (100% platinum cured silicone, ABS plastic, phthalate-free status, latex-free certifications, CE/RoHS marks).
  - Construct high-converting, educational product bundles (e.g., Beginner Starter Kit pairing a gentle vibrator with an organic water-based lubricant and antimicrobial cleaning foam).
  - Manage live inventory levels, reorder thresholds, dynamic batch pricing, and supplier margin calculations.
- **General Relationship to the Project:**
  - Utilizes the administrative catalog management interface to draft, enrich, and publish multi-brand product pages.
  - Uploads verified technical specifications, dimensional diagrams, battery life metrics, and decibel sound measurements.
  - Configures automated cross-sell rules ("Complete the Experience" pairing recommendations on cart and product pages).
  - Monitors supplier stock replenishment alerts and manages batch expiration dates for organic wellness consumables.

### Role 5: Platform Super Administrator & Business Owner
- **Role Name:** Super Administrator / Executive Business Owner
- **Who They Are:** 
  Founders, technical platform architects, and executive leaders steering commercial strategy, legal compliance, and technological infrastructure.
- **Core Purpose (Why they use the platform):**
  - Oversee holistic business performance, profit margins, operational KPIs, and privacy governance.
  - Continuously monitor the prepaid-to-COD conversion ratio, COD Return-to-Origin (RTO) rates, and Average Order Value (AOV).
  - Maintain regulatory compliance with Indian e-commerce laws, legal consumer disclosures (Legal Metrology, Consumer Protection Act), and payment gateway high-risk guidelines.
  - Manage role-based access control (RBAC), granting and revoking administrative permissions across support, catalog, and warehouse personnel.
- **General Relationship to the Project:**
  - Full access to the executive analytics dashboard (tracking gross merchandise value [GMV], RTO costs, marketing acquisition funnels, and organic search traffic).
  - Configures global financial rules, including the ₹50 COD convenience surcharge, prepaid UPI discount nudges, and payment gateway routing.
  - Audits security logs, data retention/purging policies (ensuring temporary browsing sessions and customer media are expunged according to privacy protocols).

### Role 6: Automated System Actors & External API Integrations
- **Role Name:** Automated System Actors (Machine Roles)
- **Who They Are:** 
  Autonomous microservices, background task workers, third-party logistics APIs, payment aggregator webhooks, and AI agent services operating within the ecosystem.
- **Core Entities & Purpose:**
  1. *AI Wellness Assistant Agent:* Evaluates customer queries, provides contextual anatomical and maintenance answers, and hands off complex or distressed queries to human support.
  2. *Payment Aggregator Webhooks (Cashfree / Razorpay):* Processes instant payment captures, initiates automated refunds/reversals, and sends tokenized confirmation events with neutral billing descriptors.
  3. *3PL Logistics Partner APIs (Shiprocket / Delhivery):* Consumes automated dispatch webhooks, generates Air Waybills (AWBs), and sends real-time transit status updates.
  4. *Transactional Notification Engine (MSG91 / Resend):* Dispatches silent, discreet OTPs, order confirmations, and tracking links using neutral brand templates.
  5. *Automated Anti-RTO Guardian:* Evaluates high-risk COD orders based on pin-code delivery history and triggers automated WhatsApp/SMS interactive confirmations before orders enter fulfillment.

## 🔒 Cross-Role Privacy & Operational Boundaries

1. **Strict Zero-Call Policy across all Roles:** Neither Customer Support nor Logistics staff are permitted to initiate unsolicited voice phone calls to consumers. All communication occurs via encrypted email, silent SMS, or WhatsApp OTP notifications.
2. **Neutral Identity Enforcement:** In all external-facing communications (waybills, payment aggregator merchant names, SMS headers, package exterior), the business operates under the verified neutral trade name **"Aura Logistics & Retail"**.
3. **Hygiene & Warranty Protocol Boundary:** Opened intimate wellness products are strictly non-returnable under Indian consumer hygiene standards. Customer Support only approves DOA motor/battery replacements upon verification of a customer-submitted unboxing video recorded at parcel opening.
